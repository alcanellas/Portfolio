About me

Ana López Canellas

My passion for helping others led me to user experience design. Creating design solutions that improve people’s experience fits perfectly with my wish to have a real impact through my work.

I adapt easily to different circumstances and face challenges calmly and proactively. I prioritize a stress-free environment and practice clear, assertive communication to solve problems effectively.

I love traveling to discover new cultures and immerse myself as much as I can in the everyday life of the country I’m visiting. I wouldn’t call myself a photography expert, but I’m passionate about capturing the places I visit, both digitally and on film.
