Description

Creation of a versatile, multi-brand digital product, adaptable to the company’s different brands and their needs.

What it involved

  • Discovery sessions with the client to understand the functional requirements.
  • Definition of user flows.
  • Design proposals with low- and high-fidelity prototypes.
  • Design of cross-brand components using Figma variables and modes, taking care of responsiveness and of the differences in structure and behavior between brands.
  • Follow-up and validation during development and testing.
  • Collaboration with the development teams to ensure a high-quality, consistent product.
  • Work in sprints with an agile methodology.
