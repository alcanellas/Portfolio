Description

Creation of a versatile, multi-brand digital product, adaptable to the company’s different brands and their needs.

What it involved

Design of a scalable, multi-brand digital product capable of adapting to different visual identities and functional requirements while maintaining a consistent user experience. The work covered the definition of user flows and interactions, low- and high-fidelity prototyping, and the management of a multi-brand Design System in Figma using variables. Reusable and responsive components were designed to accommodate different structures and behaviours across brands, working iteratively with stakeholders and development teams throughout sprints, implementation, and testing.
